Triage is a snapshot. Patients keep changing.

Ordinary-camera physiological change monitoring for emergency waiting rooms.

  1. Problem: triage is a snapshot while patients keep changing.
  2. Solution: an ordinary camera watches for a sustained physiological change.
  3. Action: recommend who should be reassessed, with reasons.
  4. Safety: decision support only. It does not diagnose or replace triage.

Camera → HR + RR → Confidence → Personal baseline → Sustained change → Reassessment

What this prototype does

It watches for a sustained change from a patient-specific baseline and recommends clinician reassessment. It does not assign a triage level or diagnose disease.

Decision support only. It does not diagnose or replace clinical triage.

What is real vs simulated?
  • Real: video ingestion, face tracking, facial ROIs, rPPG heart-rate estimation, respiratory-rate attempt, confidence, motion rejection, baseline, change detection.
  • Simulated: patient IDs, waiting-room labels, wait times, and some background patient tiles.
  • Guided Demo uses a prerecorded file through the same processing interfaces as live video. It never writes measurements or REASSESS from the UI.
